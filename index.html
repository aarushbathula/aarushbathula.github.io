Research

Selected Papers

Bathula, A. (2025–2026). “Housing Frictions, Spatial Misallocation, and Long-Run Growth.”
Undergraduate dissertation, University of Warwick. MATLAB, Dynare.

A dynamic model of housing search, durable housing supply, and endogenous growth, showing how housing-market frictions generate spatial misallocation, under-urbanisation, and slower long-run growth. Supervised by Dr. Arthur Galichère.

pdf
Bathula, A. (2025). “Sibling Composition and Academic Self-Perception.”
Coursework paper, University of Warwick. Stata.

Studies academic self-perception using quasi-random variation in sibling sex composition, with ordered response models and robustness analysis.

pdfcode
Bathula, A. (2025). “Country of Origin and Occupational Sorting in the United Kingdom.”
Coursework paper, University of Warwick. Stata.

Uses UK Census microdata to estimate country-of-origin effects on occupational skill placement, with a second-stage analysis of origin-country characteristics.

pdfcode
Bathula, A. (2025). “Gender Quotas and Female Political Representation in Spanish Municipal Elections.”
Coursework paper, University of Warwick. Stata.

Studies the effect of municipal gender quotas using difference-in-differences, event-study, regression discontinuity, and instrumental-variables designs.

pdfcode
Bathula, A. (2024). “Psychotropic Medication and Body Mass Index.”
Coursework paper, University of Warwick. R.

Estimates associations between prescribed psychotropic medication and body mass index using Health Survey for England microdata.

pdfcode
Bathula, A. (2025). “Technology Diffusion and Convergence in Sub-Saharan Africa.”
Coursework paper, University of Warwick. Theoretical.

Develops a growth-theoretic framework for technology diffusion and convergence under absorptive-capacity constraints.
